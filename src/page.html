Ground rules

Lineups
  • A lineup is whoever played the most. The 11 with the most offensive snaps, the 11 with the most defensive snaps, plus the kicker and punter. Regular season only.
  • Type a last name, a full name or a nickname. Megatron and Beast Mode count. Short ones like OBJ need Enter.
  • Wrong guesses cost nothing. Stuck? Tap a spot and take the clue: jersey number and college. That name then counts for half.
  • Injuries count. If the star missed ten games, the backup is the answer.
Side games
  • Four downs each. Every miss opens a clue.
  • Journeyman is one player who got around. His teams are the clue.
  • Box Score is one monster game: the real stat line and the PPR points it was worth.
  • Name That Game is a game or play famous enough to have a name, from any era.

Lineups, box scores and final scores come from nflverse (Pro Football Reference and NFL data), pulled in October 2026. The 2026 season is included so far. Your board lives in this browser only. A fan project, not affiliated with the NFL or any team.
